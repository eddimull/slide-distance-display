Seventh shows you where your trombone slide is and how in tune you are, while you play. It works with SlidePod, a small sensor on your slide that connects to your phone over Bluetooth, and listens through your phone's microphone.

See your slide, hear your pitch
• Live view: your slide position from 1st to 7th, updated as you move, with the note you're playing and how many cents sharp or flat it is.
• Pitch against the slide: Seventh knows which notes each position gives, so it can tell a note that's out of tune because of the slide from one you've lipped.
• A simple view for playing, and a detailed one with a slide trace, speed and tuning meter.

Record a take, then see what happened
• Record yourself with the slide and the sound together.
• Review the take on one timeline: where the slide went, how fast it moved, and how far each note was from in tune. Pinch to zoom in on a passage.
• Every slide move is marked: clean shifts, overshoots, and positions you stopped short of.
• Tap a note for the close-up: what went wrong in plain words, how the slide arrived, and the same note in your earlier takes of the piece.
• Play it back slower without changing the pitch, loop a note or passage, and play a reference tone to hear what in tune sounds like.
• Share a take: its audio, the note-by-note analysis as a spreadsheet, and the take itself.

Learn the slide with games
• Position Flash: a position comes up; get there fast.
• Note Flash: a note comes up on the bass clef staff, F2 to F4; find a position that plays it.
• Play the Note: play it for real, slide and all, in tune to score, with a bonus for nailing it within 10 cents.
• Combos and multipliers, personal bests, and optional leaderboards.

Made for your horn
• A short guided calibration (three slow glissandos out to 7th) teaches Seventh where your positions sit on your instrument.
• Seventh keeps learning the upper partials' tuning from your normal playing.
• Add your instruments, each with its own calibration, and keep them on your account so they follow you to a new phone.

Your SlidePod
• See its battery, including while it charges, and its connection at a glance on every screen.
• Give it a name, so you can tell it apart from others nearby, and link it to your account.
• Firmware updates install from the app over Bluetooth.

Private by design
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your recordings stay on your device unless you share them. An account is only needed for leaderboards and for keeping your instruments in the cloud; sign in with Apple or Google, and delete your account from the app at any time.

Seventh needs a SlidePod sensor for slide position. Without one, it still hears your pitch and tuning.
